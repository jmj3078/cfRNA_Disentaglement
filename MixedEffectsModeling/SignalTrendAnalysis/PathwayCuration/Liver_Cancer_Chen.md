# Liver Cancer (Chen et al.) Pathway Curation

Source: GSEA normative ranking, `normative__Liver_Cancer__Chen_et_al..csv`, FDR q<0.05 (513 terms significant before curation); pan-cancer hallmark pathways included with general (non-subtype-specific) literature support where leading-edge composition is genuine (re-screened, no additional terms qualified -- see note below).

**Cohort caution**: n=10, and this cohort previously showed a suspicious gene-level "convergence" artifact (median significant-gene count exploded 47->371 as FDR q went 0.05->0.25). The GSEA result reproduces the same pattern at the pathway level: 419/513 significant terms are NES<0 and are almost entirely driven by a shared block of ~40 proteasome-subunit genes (PSMA*/PSMB*/PSMC*/PSMD*) plus spliceosome/Complex I/ribosomal genes co-occurring as "leading edge" across dozens of nominally unrelated Reactome terms (e.g. "TCR Signaling", "Stabilization Of P53", "Fc Epsilon Receptor Signaling" all share the same PSM* leading-edge core). This is a non-specific, small-n artifact, not distinct biology, and the entire negative-NES tail was excluded from consideration below. Only the positive-NES side (94 terms, liver-secretome/metabolism dominated) was considered for curation.

## Selected Pathways

### Complement and coagulation cascades
- NES: 2.532, FDR q: 0.0000
- Literature: Complement/coagulation factor dysregulation is a documented plasma-proteome signature of cirrhosis-to-HCC progression, identified from plasma-derived extracellular vesicles in a proteomic biomarker study (PMID 39011654), supporting direct detectability in blood-derived cfRNA. Same term/citation independently kept in the Roskams-Hieter cohort curation (cross-cohort replication, see below).
- Lead genes (top ~10): FGB, FGA, FGG, C9, KNG1, PLAU, PLG, VTN, C4BPB, SERPING1

### YAP1- And WWTR1 (TAZ)-stimulated Gene Expression R-HSA-2032785
- NES: 1.984, FDR q: 0.0043
- Literature: Hippo pathway inactivation with downstream YAP1/TAZ(WWTR1) hyperactivation is a well-established HCC driver mechanism (targeted review, PMID 39092548; HBV-associated Hippo inactivation via lncRNA RP11-40C6.2/YAP1 ubiquitylation, PMID 36643034). Conceptually matches Roskams-Hieter's kept "Signaling By Hippo R-HSA-2028269" (same WWTR1/YAP1 axis, downstream transcriptional output here vs. upstream signaling there).
- Lead genes (top ~10): RUNX2, WWTR1, YAP1, HIPK2, TEAD4, TBX5, HIPK1

### Binding And Uptake Of Ligands By Scavenger Receptors R-HSA-2173782
- NES: 2.082, FDR q: 0.0017
- Literature: Hepatocyte scavenger-receptor-mediated lipoprotein uptake (SR-BI, LDLR family) is documented liver-specific biology relevant to lipid handling in hepatoma cells (PMID 17905649), and CD36-mediated lipid/iron handling is implicated in early-stage HCC immune dysfunction (PMID 40037690). Same term kept in Roskams-Hieter cohort curation with the same citations (cross-cohort replication).
- Lead genes (top ~10): ALB, HSP90B1, APOB, AMBP, HPX, CALR, APOA1, JCHAIN, APOE, HYOU1

## Dropped candidates (GSEA-significant, no adequate literature support)
- Entire NES<0 tail (419 terms, e.g. "Metabolism Of RNA", "Proteasome", "mRNA Splicing - Major Pathway", "Complex I Biogenesis", "TCR Signaling", "Stabilization Of P53"): all share a ~40-gene proteasome/spliceosome/OXPHOS leading-edge core -- this is the small-n convergence artifact flagged for this cohort, not distinct disease biology. Excluded en masse rather than term-by-term.
- Regulation Of IGF Transport And Uptake By IGFBPs R-HSA-381426: NES 2.614 -- top hit by NES but driven by the same generic hepatic secretome block (ALB/APOB/TF/FGA/FGG) as several other terms below; no HCC-specific (vs. generic liver-organ-of-origin) literature found for this exact IGFBP-transport framing.
- Post-translational Protein Phosphorylation R-HSA-8957275: NES 2.602 -- >90% Lead_genes overlap with IGFBP-transport term above (ALB/APOB/TF/FGA/FGG core); redundant generic secretome signal.
- Glycosaminoglycan biosynthesis / Glycosaminoglycan Metabolism / HS-GAG Metabolism: NES 2.52/2.08/1.88 -- glypican family (GPC1/GPC5/GPC2/GPC6) is in the leading edge, and glypican-3 (GPC3, a different family member) is a well-established HCC biomarker (PMID 28621802), but GPC3 itself is not in this pathway's leading-edge genes -- too indirect a link to keep.
- Bile secretion / Retinol metabolism / Aspirin ADME / Drug ADME / Metabolism of xenobiotics by cytochrome P450 / Xenobiotics: NES 1.9-2.4 -- generic hepatocyte drug-/xenobiotic-metabolism organ-of-origin signal (CYP/UGT/ADH gene families), not disease-specific; no HCC-specific citation found beyond "liver tissue is present."
- Plasma Lipoprotein Remodeling/Assembly / Cholesterol metabolism / Chylomicron Assembly / HDL Remodeling: NES 1.8-2.2 -- generic hepatocyte lipoprotein-synthesis signal (APOA/APOB/APOC/APOE core); searched literature returned only generic MASLD/lipid-metabolism reviews, no HCC-specific disease link.
- Extracellular Matrix Organization R-HSA-1474244: NES 1.860 -- broad 287-gene set; literature search returned no HCC-cfRNA-specific citation, only generic fibrosis biology; too large/nonspecific to keep without a sharper citation.
- Platelet Degranulation R-HSA-114608 / Response To Elevated Platelet Cytosolic Ca2+ R-HSA-76005 (identical leading edge, kept as one): NES 1.855/1.808 -- plausible cirrhosis-associated coagulopathy story but literature search found no HCC/cfRNA-specific citation, only generic platelet biology; dropped for lack of a citable disease-specific link.
- Regulation Of Complement Cascade R-HSA-977606 / Complement Cascade R-HSA-166658 (>70% overlap with each other and with kept "Complement and coagulation cascades"): NES 1.974/1.763 -- near-duplicates of the kept KEGG complement/coagulation term; redundant.
- Common Pathway Of Fibrin Clot Formation R-HSA-140875 / Formation Of Fibrin Clot (Clotting Cascade) R-HSA-140877: NES 2.172/1.994 -- 100% Lead_genes overlap with each other and high overlap with kept "Complement and coagulation cascades"; also flagged as generic organ-of-origin coagulation-factor signal (not disease-specific) in the Roskams-Hieter curation.

## Relaxed-criterion re-screen note
All 94 positive-NES significant terms were re-screened for pan-cancer hallmark candidates (proliferation/cell-cycle, EMT, angiogenesis, invasion, apoptosis evasion, immune evasion, Rho/Ras GTPase signaling, senescence bypass). Only the already-kept YAP1/WWTR1(TAZ) term matched a genuine hallmark theme with clean leading-edge composition; the remaining 91 non-kept terms are dominated by liver metabolism/transport/ADME/organ-of-origin machinery (bile secretion, retinol/glycosaminoglycan/lipoprotein metabolism, SLC transporters, drug ADME, ion channels), not disease-hallmark biology, so none newly qualify. Kept-pathway count is unchanged at 3. This cohort's severe proteasome/spliceosome leading-edge convergence artifact (419/513 significant terms, NES<0 tail) is untouched by the relaxed rule -- the relaxation only widens what counts as adequately-cited biology on the clean positive-NES side, it does not touch the artifact-exclusion check (>80% PSM*/ribosomal/spliceosome leading-edge dominance is still disqualifying regardless of citation).

## Cross-cohort note
Of the 3 pathways kept here, 2 directly replicate Roskams-Hieter's kept list: "Complement and coagulation cascades" (identical term name in both) and "Binding And Uptake Of Ligands By Scavenger Receptors R-HSA-2173782" (identical term, identical citations). The third, YAP1/WWTR1-stimulated gene expression, is mechanistically the downstream half of Roskams-Hieter's kept "Signaling By Hippo R-HSA-2028269" (same WWTR1/YAP1 axis). This is a meaningfully positive cross-cohort signal: despite Chen's small n=10 and the severe proteasome-driven convergence artifact dominating the negative-NES tail, the surviving positive-NES, literature-supported pathways land on essentially the same three biological themes (complement/coagulation, scavenger-receptor lipid uptake, Hippo/YAP-TAZ) independently found in the much larger Roskams-Hieter cohort. This overlap looks like genuine shared HCC signal rather than batch/small-n noise -- but the fact that 82% of Chen's raw significant terms had to be discarded en masse as an artifact (vs. term-by-term curation in Roskams-Hieter) means this cohort's pathway list should be treated as weaker/exploratory evidence and not weighted equally with Roskams-Hieter's in any downstream patient-level Sankey narrative.
